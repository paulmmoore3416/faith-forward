Donation Guidelines

  • Clothing should be clean and wearable. Underwear and socks must be new.
  • Food must be sealed, non-perishable, and unexpired. Pop-top cans are best because many veterans have no can opener.
  • Hygiene items should be new and sealed. Travel sizes are ideal.
  • Animal food should be sealed. Gently used leashes, collars, crates, and coats are welcome.
  • Religious items of every denomination are welcome and handled reverently.
  • We cannot accept weapons, alcohol, medications, or opened food.
