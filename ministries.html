What We Do

Ministries & Services

Practical help and spiritual care, delivered where veterans are.

Ministry 1

🎒 Street Outreach & Supply Distribution

Our teams go to the veterans: camps, underpasses, parks, transit centers, meal sites, and parking lots. We carry blessed backpacks stocked with clothing, hygiene supplies, food, water, socks, and prayer items, plus food and gear for their animals.

  • Weekly outreach routes across St. Louis City and County
  • Seasonal drives: Winter Survival Backpacks, Summer Heat Relief, Back-to-School for veteran families
  • Every contact logged so we can follow up within 7 days

Ministry 2

✝️ Spiritual Care & Chaplaincy

Led by an ordained deacon, our spiritual care ministry offers prayer, scripture, pastoral conversation, help confronting moral injury, grief support, and referral to the sacraments through local parishes. Veterans of every faith tradition are welcome; we connect people with clergy of their own denomination on request.

  • Street-side prayer and blessing (for veterans and their animals)
  • Bibles, rosaries, devotionals, and prayer items for every denomination
  • Memorial prayer services and funeral support for veterans who die without family
  • Annual Blessing of the Animals on the Feast of St. Francis (October 4)

Ministry 3

🐕 K9 Program

Food, preventive care, vaccines, gear, and professional training for veterans' dogs, including emotional-support and psychiatric-service task training, so a veteran never has to choose between their dog and a bed.

Learn about the K9 Program

Ministry 4

🧭 Resource Navigation

Using the STL Veterans Hub resource map, we connect veterans to VA healthcare, HUD-VASH housing, SSVF rapid re-housing, benefits claims (through accredited VSOs), employment, legal aid, ID replacement, food, and recovery programs. We do warm handoffs: we make the call with you and go with you when we can.

Ministry 5

💙 Mental Health Partnerships

Faith Forward is not a clinical provider. We partner with licensed mental-health providers, Vet Centers, the VA, and outreach programs to connect veterans with PTSD, depression, substance use, and suicide-risk care quickly and respectfully.

Ministry 6

👨‍👩‍👧 Veteran Family Support

Spouses, children, and caregivers serve too. We provide supplies, holiday support, prayer, and referrals to family-focused services, and we offer pastoral support to Gold Star families.
