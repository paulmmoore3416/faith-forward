Why Dogs Matter in Veteran Outreach

A dog gives a veteran on the street warmth, security, companionship, and purpose. But many shelters and housing programs cannot take animals, so veterans often turn down a bed rather than abandon their dog. Untrained or unhealthy dogs also get veterans turned away.

Faith Forward closes that gap. We help dogs become healthy, well-mannered, documented companions, and where appropriate, trained emotional-support or psychiatric service dogs. That opens doors to housing and care.

Our program uses methods drawn from military working dog (MWD) and service-dog training, adapted for veterans in every living situation.

The Training Pathway

Phase 0

Handler Foundations

Body language, learning theory, safety, the law, faith and stewardship.

Phase 1

Foundation Obedience

Sit, down, place, recall, loose leash, leave it, out, muzzle, socialization.

Phase 2

Advanced (MWD Standard)

Off-leash control, distance work, noise, obstacles, CGC.

Phase 3

Support & Service

Deep pressure therapy, anxiety interruption, nightmare wake-up, retrieval.

Phase 4

Detection

Odor imprinting and systematic searches using sport odors.

Phase 4

Tracking

Footstep tracking and person trailing.

Phase 6

Protection (Restricted)

Control-first protection with certified helpers, evaluation and legal briefing.

Phase 7

Train-the-Trainer

Veterans certified to train other veterans and their dogs.
