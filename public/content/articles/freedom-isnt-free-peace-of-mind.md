# Freedom Isn't Free, and Neither Is Peace of Mind

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · July 4, 2027 · Week 36*

> **"And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."**
> John 8:32 (KJV)

Happy Independence Day. Today Americans celebrate the freedom declared in 1776 and defended ever since by those who served.

## The Cost

"Freedom isn't free" is printed on countless bumper stickers. For veterans, it's more than a slogan. The cost is the friend who didn't come back, the knees that don't bend, the nightmares, the family time lost to deployments. Thank veterans today, and then help carry the cost they still pay.

## Another Kind of Freedom

Political freedom matters. But many veterans live in a free country and still feel imprisoned: by addiction, guilt, trauma, anger, or despair. Jesus offered a deeper freedom: *"If the Son therefore shall make you free, ye shall be free indeed"* (John 8:36).

That freedom comes through truth: telling the truth about our wounds, receiving the truth of God's forgiveness, and living in the truth that we're loved.

## Liberty and Responsibility

The Founders believed freedom required virtue. Freedom without responsibility becomes chaos. Veterans understand that: discipline made us free to accomplish the mission. The same is true spiritually.

## Celebrate Wisely

Celebrate with joy, and remember your veteran neighbors when the fireworks start. Invite a lonely veteran to your cookout. Pray for those serving overseas today.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*God of liberty, thank You for this nation and for those who paid the price of its freedom. Bless our veterans and those serving now. Free every soul still in chains of addiction, guilt, or despair. Make us free indeed in Christ, and make us worthy of our liberty. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

If a veteran is struggling today, the **Veterans Crisis Line** is open 24/7: 988, press 1. Holidays can be hard.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

July 4th dog checklist: indoors before dark, ID tag on, microchip registered, safe den ready, white noise on, and a frozen Kong for distraction.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Invite a veteran to your celebration, and say thank you to someone who paid the price.
