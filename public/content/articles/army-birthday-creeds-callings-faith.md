# The Army Turns 252: Creeds, Callings, and a Soldier's Faith

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · June 13, 2027 · Week 33*

> **"I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith."**
> 2 Timothy 4:7 (KJV)

On June 14, 1775, the Continental Congress established the Continental Army. This June 14, the U.S. Army turns **252**, and it's also Flag Day. Happy birthday to every soldier, past and present.

## The Warrior Ethos

At the heart of the Soldier's Creed is the Warrior Ethos:

> *I will always place the mission first. I will never accept defeat. I will never quit. I will never leave a fallen comrade.*

Those lines got many soldiers through basic training, combat, and loss. They can carry you through life after the Army too.

## The Creed After the Uniform

- **Mission first:** find a new mission worth your life: your family, your sobriety, serving others, serving God.
- **Never accept defeat:** relapse, eviction, and setbacks aren't defeat unless you quit.
- **Never quit:** keep showing up.
- **Never leave a fallen comrade:** check on your battle buddies. Some of them are falling right now.

## The Creed and the Cross

Paul wrote like a soldier: *"I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith."* Jesus never left a fallen comrade; He came looking for the lost. The Warrior Ethos is a shadow of the Gospel.

Our founder served three combat tours in Iraq. Faith Forward is, in a sense, his way of keeping that last line of the Warrior Ethos: *I will never leave a fallen comrade.*

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord of hosts, bless the United States Army on its birthday. Protect soldiers serving today. Heal Army veterans in body, mind, and spirit. Help every soldier keep the creed they swore: mission first, never quit, never leave a fallen comrade. And let us finish our course and keep the faith. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Army veterans: **Army Emergency Relief** helps active-duty soldiers and retirees with emergencies. Veterans of all branches can get claim help free through VSOs like DAV, VFW, and American Legion.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

The Army's first official K9 program began in 1942. Today, military working dogs train at Joint Base San Antonio-Lackland. Their training principles inspire Phase 2 of our K9 curriculum.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Call an Army buddy and say happy birthday. Then recite the Warrior Ethos and pick one line to live this week.
