# St. Francis and the Blessing of the Animals

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · October 3, 2027 · Week 49*

> **"O LORD, thou preservest man and beast."**
> Psalm 36:6 (KJV)

October 4 is the **Feast of St. Francis of Assisi**, and churches around the world hold a **Blessing of the Animals**. For Faith Forward, it's one of the most important days of the year.

## The Saint of Creation

Francis was a wealthy young man who became a soldier, went to war, and was taken prisoner. After illness and a profound conversion, he gave up everything to live in poverty and serve the poor and lepers. He called the sun his brother and the moon his sister. Stories tell of him preaching to birds and taming a wolf that terrorized the town of Gubbio by making peace between the wolf and the townspeople.

Francis was a former soldier, a man who had been a prisoner, and a man who lived with the poor. He's a natural patron for our ministry.

## Why Bless Animals?

God made animals and called them good. He saved them in the ark, and Psalm 36 says He preserves "man and beast." Blessing an animal is a way of thanking God for His gift and asking His protection over it. For a veteran whose dog is family, a blessing is deeply meaningful.

## Our Blessing Day

Faith Forward holds a **Blessing of the Animals** for veterans and their pets, with free food, supplies, basic health checks, and training tips. Every dog is blessed; every handler is prayed over.

## The Wolf of Gubbio

The story of the wolf of Gubbio says Francis didn't kill the wolf. He made peace. Some of our veterans' dogs have rough reputations. With training and love, the "wolf" becomes a guardian of peace.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Blessed are You, Lord God, Creator of all living things. Bless these animals and all who care for them. Keep them healthy and safe. May they continue to be companions, comforters, and guardians, and may we always care for them as You care for us. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Many St. Louis parishes hold Blessing of the Animals services around October 4. Faith Forward's blessing includes free supplies for veterans' animals.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Blessing-day manners: practice calm greetings and a solid sit-stay before the event. A crowd of excited dogs is a great socialization opportunity, at a comfortable distance.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Bring your dog to a blessing, and donate animal supplies for veterans' dogs.
