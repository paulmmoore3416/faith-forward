# Why Counting Matters: The Point-in-Time Count

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · January 24, 2027 · Week 13*

> **"What man of you, having an hundred sheep, if he lose one of them, doth not leave the ninety and nine... and go after that which is lost?"**
> Luke 15:4 (KJV)

Every January, communities across the U.S. conduct the **Point-in-Time (PIT) Count**, a one-night snapshot of people experiencing homelessness, both sheltered and unsheltered. Volunteers walk streets, parks, and camps in the cold to count and survey people.

## Why It Matters

PIT data shapes how federal, state, and local funding is distributed. It helps agencies see trends, measure progress on veteran homelessness, and plan services. **If someone isn't counted, they're harder to help.**

## The Shepherd Counts

Jesus told of a shepherd who counted his sheep, noticed one missing, and left the ninety-nine to find it. God knows every one of His children by name. The PIT count is an imperfect human echo of that divine attention: every person matters enough to be counted.

## Veterans and the Count

Surveys ask whether a person has served in the military. Some veterans don't identify as veterans, especially if they served briefly, were discharged under difficult circumstances, or never thought of themselves as "real" veterans. Volunteers can gently ask, "**Have you ever served in the military?**" That one question can open doors to VA programs.

## How to Volunteer

The St. Louis City and County Continuums of Care organize the count. Volunteers usually attend a short training and go out in teams. Faith Forward brings backpacks and dog supplies so every veteran contact receives something tangible, along with a prayer if they want one.

## After the Count

The count is a snapshot, not a solution. Faith Forward follows up with veterans we meet, connecting them to VA homeless programs, SSVF, and housing, and their dogs to food and vet care.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Good Shepherd, You know every name and every face. As we count, let us see each person as You see them: beloved, valuable, worth finding. Lead the lost home, and use our small efforts to bring big changes. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**VA Homeless Programs**: any veteran experiencing homelessness can walk into Jefferson Barracks or John Cochran VA and ask for the homeless program, or call **877-424-3838**.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

During outreach, always ask before approaching someone's dog, approach from the side at an angle, and let the dog sniff the back of your hand. Offering water or food for the dog is one of the fastest ways to build trust with the owner.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Sign up to volunteer for the Point-in-Time Count, or donate backpacks and dog food for count-night distribution.
