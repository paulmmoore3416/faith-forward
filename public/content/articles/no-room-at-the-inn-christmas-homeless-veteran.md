# No Room at the Inn: Christmas and the Homeless Veteran

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · December 20, 2026 · Week 8*

> **"And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger; because there was no room for them in the inn."**
> Luke 2:7 (KJV)

"No room." Two words in the Christmas story that most of us read past. A pregnant young woman and a tired carpenter went door to door in a crowded town and heard the same answer again and again. So the Son of God was born where the animals slept.

## God Chose the Margins

God could have arrived in a palace. He chose a stable, among animals, in a family far from home. Within months the Holy Family would flee to Egypt as refugees. From the first night, Jesus identified with the displaced.

That means every veteran sleeping in a car tonight, every family doubled up in a relative's basement, every man in a tent with his dog, is in the company of the Holy Family.

## Making Room

The question of Christmas is not only "Where was there no room?" It is "**Where will I make room?**"

- **Room at your table**: invite a veteran who would otherwise be alone.
- **Room in your budget**: sponsor a backpack, a family's groceries, or a winter coat.
- **Room in your parish**: host a drive, open a warming space, start a veterans ministry.
- **Room in your heart**: let go of the stereotype of "the homeless guy" and learn one veteran's name and story.

## The Animals at the Manger

Tradition places an ox and a donkey at the manger, the first creatures to keep the newborn King warm. It is a fitting picture for our K9 ministry. The animals beside our veterans keep them warm too, and at Christmas we bring food, coats, and toys for them as well.

## Christmas With Faith Forward

This week our teams distribute warm clothing, hygiene supplies, food, gifts for veteran families, and treats and toys for their animals. Every item is blessed. Every veteran hears the same message the shepherds heard: **"Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy."**

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord Jesus, born where there was no room, make room in our hearts and homes this Christmas. Be close to every veteran without a home tonight, every family far from each other, every soul who feels shut out. Let them hear the angels' song. Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**St. Patrick Center – SSVF** (800 N. Tucker Blvd, 314-802-0700) provides Supportive Services for Veteran Families, including rapid re-housing and homelessness prevention for veteran households.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Holiday hazards for dogs: chocolate, xylitol (in sugar-free candy and gum), tinsel, ribbon, lilies, and electrical cords. Keep gifts with food in them off the floor. A stuffed, frozen Kong keeps a dog busy during gatherings.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Make room for one: invite, sponsor, or deliver. Then pray Luke 2 with your family on Christmas Eve.
