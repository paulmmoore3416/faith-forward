# Lent for Warriors: Discipline, Sacrifice, and Grace

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · February 7, 2027 · Week 15*

> **"Rend your heart, and not your garments, and turn unto the LORD your God: for he is gracious and merciful."**
> Joel 2:13 (KJV)

This Wednesday, February 10, the Church begins **Lent** with ashes and the ancient words: *"Remember that you are dust, and to dust you shall return."* For forty days, Christians prepare for Easter through **prayer, fasting, and almsgiving**.

## Warriors Understand Discipline

Veterans already know what Lent asks. Basic training was a forty-day-and-then-some exercise in stripping away comfort to build something stronger. PT at 0500. Inspections. Doing hard things on purpose because they made you ready when it counted.

Lent is spiritual PT. It isn't about earning God's love; you can't. It's about clearing out what crowds Him out.

## The Three Disciplines

**Prayer** is your daily check-in with your Commander. Try praying the same short prayer morning and night all Lent, perhaps the Jesus Prayer: *"Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner."*

**Fasting** builds self-control. Catholics fast on Ash Wednesday and Good Friday and abstain from meat on Fridays. Many people give up something more: alcohol, social media, energy drinks. If you're in recovery, a Lenten fast can strengthen sobriety. Work with your sponsor and doctor.

**Almsgiving** turns sacrifice into service. Take what you save by fasting and give it to someone in need. Pack a backpack for a veteran. Buy a bag of dog food for a veteran's pup.

## Ashes and Honesty

Ashes are honest. They say: *I'm mortal, I fall short, and I need God.* That honesty is freeing for veterans who carry guilt. Lent ends at an empty tomb. The dust is not the end of the story.

## A Faith Forward Lent

Join our **40 Days, 40 Backpacks** challenge: individuals, families, and parishes each pack one backpack per week of Lent for veterans on the street.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, I am dust, and You love me anyway. This Lent, strip away what keeps me from You. Strengthen my prayer, discipline my appetites, and open my hands to the poor. Lead me through the desert to the empty tomb. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**VA SARRTP (Substance Abuse Residential Rehabilitation Treatment Program)** at Jefferson Barracks provides residential treatment for veterans. Ask any VA provider for a referral.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Dogs need "fasting" from overfeeding too. Most pets are overweight. Measure meals, count training treats as part of the daily ration, and aim to feel the ribs easily under a thin layer of fat.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Pick one prayer, one fast, and one act of giving for Lent. Write them down and tell someone.
