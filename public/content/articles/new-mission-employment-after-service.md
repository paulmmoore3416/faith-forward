# A New Mission: Employment After Service

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · August 1, 2027 · Week 40*

> **"Whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men."**
> Colossians 3:23 (KJV)

In the military, your job was clear and your purpose was obvious. Civilian work can feel like trading a mission for a paycheck. It doesn't have to.

## Work Is a Calling

Scripture treats work as dignity, not drudgery. Adam was given work in the garden before the Fall. Jesus was a carpenter. Paul made tents. Whatever you do, do it "heartily, as to the Lord." That turns any honest job into ministry.

## Translate Your Skills

Employers often don't understand military jargon. Translate it:

- "Squad leader" → supervised and trained a team of 9, responsible for $X million in equipment
- "Convoy commander" → planned and led logistics operations under pressure
- "Armorer" → maintained inventory and compliance for sensitive equipment

The VA, Department of Labor, and many nonprofits offer free resume help and skills translators.

## St. Louis Resources

- **Missouri Job Center** locations, including St. Louis City: priority service for veterans
- **VR&E (Chapter 31)**: Veteran Readiness and Employment for veterans with service-connected disabilities, including training and job placement
- **Employment Connection**: Homeless Veterans Reintegration Program
- **Veterans Community Project, The Mission Continues**, and other groups

## Barriers We Help With

A record, a gap in work history, no ID, no phone, no transportation, no clothes for interviews. Faith Forward helps with ID and documents, interview clothing, hygiene, rides, and prayer.

## Employers: Hire a Veteran

Veterans bring discipline, leadership, and loyalty. Consider veteran-hiring initiatives and second-chance hiring.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, You gave us work as a blessing. Open doors of good employment for veterans looking for a job. Help employers see their value. Give every veteran a mission worth getting up for, and let us work heartily, as unto You. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Missouri Job Center – St. Louis City** (1520 Market St., 314-589-8000) and **VR&E** at the St. Louis VA Regional Office (800-827-1000).

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Dog-related jobs are a real pathway: kennel tech, groomer, dog walker, trainer, vet assistant, shelter worker. Completing our K9 curriculum and Train-the-Trainer phase can be a stepping stone.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Update your resume with translated skills, or, if you're an employer, interview a veteran this month.
