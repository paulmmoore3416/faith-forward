# Addiction and Recovery: There Is No Pit So Deep

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · February 28, 2027 · Week 18*

> **"He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings."**
> Psalm 40:2 (KJV)

Corrie ten Boom's sister Betsie said it in a Nazi concentration camp: *"There is no pit so deep that God's love is not deeper still."* Many veterans know the pit of addiction from the inside, and so does our founder.

## Why Veterans Struggle

Veterans may turn to alcohol, opioids, or other drugs to manage pain, sleep, nightmares, or the loneliness of life after the unit. Military culture often normalized heavy drinking. Chronic pain from injuries led some to prescription opioids. None of this is a moral failure. Addiction is a disease, and recovery is possible.

## The Road Back

Recovery rarely happens alone. It usually includes:

1. **Honesty**: admitting the problem to yourself, God, and another person.
2. **Medical help**: detox can be dangerous, especially from alcohol or benzodiazepines. Get medical supervision.
3. **Treatment**: outpatient or residential programs, including VA programs.
4. **Community**: AA, NA, Celebrate Recovery, SMART Recovery, or veteran-specific groups.
5. **Faith**: a Higher Power isn't an abstraction for us; His name is Jesus, and He walks the steps with you.

## Relapse Isn't the End

Relapse happens to many people in recovery. It's not a reason to quit trying. Peter denied Christ three times and was restored. Get back up, call your sponsor, and start again today.

## How the Church Can Help

Host recovery meetings. Offer sober fellowship. Don't shame. Celebrate milestones. Treat the person in recovery as a full member of the body of Christ, because they are.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord Jesus, You lift us out of the pit and set our feet on rock. Strengthen every veteran fighting addiction today. Give them one more sober hour, then one more day. Surround them with honest friends. When they fall, lift them without shame. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**SAMHSA National Helpline**: **800-662-4357**, free, confidential, 24/7 treatment referral. **VA SARRTP** at Jefferson Barracks offers residential treatment for veterans.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

A dog can be a powerful recovery partner: walks give structure to the day, and caring for the dog adds purpose. Build a daily routine around your dog's needs (feeding, training, walks) to anchor your own.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

If you're struggling, tell one person today. If you're in recovery, call someone newer than you this week.
