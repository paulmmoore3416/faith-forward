# Mental Health Partners: Why We Link, Not Replace

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · May 2, 2027 · Week 27*

> **"Honour a physician with the honour due unto him... for the Lord hath created him."**
> Sirach 38:1 (KJV)

May is Mental Health Awareness Month. It's a good time to explain one of Faith Forward's core convictions: **we link veterans to licensed mental-health care; we don't replace it.**

## Faith and Medicine Are Allies

Some Christians worry that seeing a therapist shows a lack of faith. Scripture disagrees. Luke, who wrote a Gospel and Acts, was a physician. The book of Sirach (in Catholic and Orthodox Bibles) tells us to honor the physician, "for the Lord hath created him." God heals through prayer and through people trained to heal.

## What Faith Forward Provides

- **Spiritual care**: prayer, scripture, pastoral conversation, help with moral injury and grief
- **Practical support**: transportation, paperwork, dog care during appointments, supplies
- **Community**: brotherhood, fellowship, and follow-up
- **Warm handoffs** to clinical partners

## What Our Partners Provide

Licensed providers, Vet Centers, the VA, and community mental-health centers provide **assessment, diagnosis, therapy, and medication management**: evidence-based care such as Cognitive Processing Therapy and Prolonged Exposure for PTSD, treatment for depression and substance use, and crisis services.

## What a Warm Handoff Looks Like

A cold referral is a phone number on a card. A **warm handoff** is: *"Let's call together right now. I'll drive you Tuesday. I'll watch your dog while you're in there. I'll check in Wednesday."* It makes a massive difference.

## Calling All Providers

If you're a St. Louis-area counselor, clinician, or program serving veterans, we'd love to partner. We sign a simple MOU covering referral steps, consent, and crisis protocol.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, bless the counselors, psychologists, psychiatrists, social workers, and peer specialists who care for veterans' minds. Give them wisdom, endurance, and compassion. Unite faith and medicine in the work of healing. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**St. Louis Vet Center** (314-845-8580): readjustment counseling for eligible veterans and families. **VA Mental Health** at John Cochran and Jefferson Barracks offers same-day mental-health access for urgent needs.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Dogs can make therapy easier. Ask whether your provider allows a calm, well-mannered dog in sessions. Even if not, a walk with your dog afterward helps decompress.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Providers: reach out about partnership. Everyone else: share this article to help end the stigma of seeking care.
