# Love That Stays: Veteran Marriages and Families

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · February 14, 2027 · Week 16*

> **"Charity suffereth long, and is kind... Beareth all things, believeth all things, hopeth all things, endureth all things."**
> 1 Corinthians 13:4,7 (KJV)

Valentine's Day is about romance, but the love that holds veteran families together looks less like flowers and more like 1 Corinthians 13: **patient, kind, enduring**.

## The Strain Is Real

Military families live through repeated separations, the fear of deployment, and homecomings to someone changed by war. After service, PTSD, TBI, chronic pain, unemployment, and substance use can strain even strong marriages. Spouses often become caregivers without anyone asking if they're okay.

## What Helps

1. **Talk about the hard stuff, carefully.** Set a weekly time to check in when you're both calm.
2. **Learn each other's triggers.** Agree on a code word or signal for "I need a break."
3. **Get help early.** Couples counseling isn't a sign of failure; it's preventive maintenance.
4. **Pray together**, even for one minute at night.
5. **Protect date time**, even if it's coffee and a walk with the dog.
6. **Caregivers, care for yourselves.** You can't pour from an empty cup.

## For Families Already Broken

Many veterans we serve have lost marriages and contact with children. Reconciliation isn't always possible or safe, but healing is. We walk with veterans toward forgiveness, responsible fatherhood and motherhood, and, where possible, restored relationships.

## The Covenant God Keeps

When human love fails, God's doesn't. *"I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee"* (Hebrews 13:5). That covenant can become the foundation for rebuilding every other one.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*God of covenant love, bless veteran marriages and families. Heal the wounds that deployments and war left in our homes. Give spouses patience, caregivers rest, and children security. Where love is broken, make it new. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**VA Caregiver Support Program** (Caregiver Support Line 855-260-3274) helps those caring for veterans. **Vet Centers** offer marriage and family counseling for eligible veterans.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Family dog training tip: everyone in the house should use the **same words** for commands. Write them on the fridge (Sit, Down, Place, Here, Leave It, Out, Free). Consistency is love to a dog.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Spouses: plan one simple date this week. Supporters: sponsor a care package for a veteran caregiver.
