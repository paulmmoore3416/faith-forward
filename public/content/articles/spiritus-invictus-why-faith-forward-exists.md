# Spiritus Invictus: Why Faith Forward Exists

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · November 1, 2026 · Week 1*

> **"Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me."**
> Matthew 25:40 (KJV)

Today is All Saints' Day, the day the Church remembers the great cloud of witnesses who ran their race and finished it. It is a fitting day to begin. Saints are not people who never fell. They are people who got back up because grace would not let them stay down.

That is the story behind **Faith Forward Foundation**.

## A Founder Who Has Walked These Streets

Our founder, **The Reverend Paul Moore**, served three combat tours in Iraq and came home a disabled veteran. Like too many of his brothers and sisters, he found that coming home was a battlefield of its own. He went through homelessness, addiction, and the justice system on the streets of St. Louis. He knows what it is to have a dog as your only family, a backpack as your only closet, and a prayer as your only plan.

He also knows that grace finds people there. Today he serves as an ordained minister, called to the poor and forgotten. He is the creator and founder of **STL Veterans Hub** (stlveteranshub.com), which maps every veteran resource in the region, and now of Faith Forward, which takes that map, and the Gospel, to the street.

## What We Do

Faith Forward provides **assistance, resources, supplies, and religious faith-based services** to displaced veterans, their families, and veterans in need across the St. Louis area. In practice, that means:

- **Street outreach** with blessed backpacks, clothing, hygiene supplies, food, and water
- **Spiritual care**: prayer, scripture, pastoral conversation, and help with moral injury, for veterans of every faith or none
- **Resource navigation** to VA healthcare, housing, benefits, employment, and recovery, with warm handoffs instead of phone numbers on a napkin
- **Mental-health partnerships** that link veterans to licensed providers quickly
- **A first-of-its-kind K9 program** for the dogs who walk beside unhoused veterans

## Why the Dogs?

Spend a week doing street outreach in St. Louis and you notice it: a large share of veterans living outside have an animal with them. That dog is warmth on a January night, an alarm system, a reason to get up, and often the only one who never left. Many shelters and housing programs cannot take animals, so veterans turn down a bed rather than abandon their companion.

Faith Forward refuses to make anyone choose. We feed, heal, and train both ends of the leash so that veterans and their dogs can move forward together.

## Spiritus Invictus

Our crest carries the Latin phrase *Spiritus Invictus*, "the unconquered spirit." The shield is the armor of God. The gold cross is Christ at the center. The flame is the Holy Spirit and the fire that war could not put out. Every veteran we meet has been wounded. None of them has been conquered.

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Veterans Crisis Line**: call **988 and press 1**, text **838255**, or chat at VeteransCrisisLine.net. It is free, confidential, and available 24/7 for veterans, service members, and the people who love them. You do not need to be enrolled in VA care to use it.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Every Faith Forward dog starts with one skill: **name recognition**. Say your dog's name once, cheerfully. The instant they look at you, say "YES" and give a small treat. Ten repetitions, twice a day. That one-second look is the foundation of everything else.
