# Twenty-Six Years After 9/11: The Generation That Answered

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · September 12, 2027 · Week 46*

> **"The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit."**
> Psalm 34:18 (KJV)

Twenty-six years ago, on September 11, 2001, nearly 3,000 people were killed in the attacks on New York, Washington, and the plane that crashed in Pennsylvania. Firefighters, police officers, and ordinary people ran toward danger to save others.

## The Generation That Answered

In the years that followed, millions of Americans served in the post-9/11 wars, many volunteering because of that day. They deployed to Afghanistan and Iraq, often multiple times. Our founder served three tours in Iraq.

That generation came home with high rates of PTSD, TBI, toxic exposure, and moral injury, and many have struggled. We also lost too many to suicide after they came home.

## Remember Them All

On this anniversary, remember:

- The victims of the attacks
- First responders who died that day and since, from related illnesses
- Service members killed in the wars that followed
- Veterans who came home and died from their invisible wounds
- Families who still carry the grief

## Where Was God?

Many ask where God was on 9/11. Psalm 34 answers: **near the brokenhearted**. He was in the stairwells with the firefighters, in the hearts of the passengers who fought back, and in the arms of everyone who comforted the grieving. God doesn't always prevent evil, but He never abandons us in it.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, on this day we remember. Grant eternal rest to the victims of September 11, to first responders, and to all who died in the wars that followed. Heal the post-9/11 generation of veterans. Be near the brokenhearted. Turn evil to good, and bring peace. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Post-9/11 veterans: you may be eligible for expanded VA health care under the PACT Act. Call **800-698-2411** or visit VA.gov.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Search-and-rescue dogs worked at Ground Zero and the Pentagon after 9/11. Their legacy lives on in every SAR and tracking dog today, including our Phase 4 tracking curriculum.
