# Starting Over: A New Year Battle Plan for Veterans

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · December 27, 2026 · Week 9*

> **"Remember ye not the former things, neither consider the things of old. Behold, I will do a new thing; now it shall spring forth."**
> Isaiah 43:18-19 (KJV)

New Year's resolutions fail because they are wishes, not plans. Veterans know the difference. You never rolled out the gate with a vague hope; you had an operations order. Let's write one for your year.

## 1. Mission Statement

One sentence: *what are you fighting for this year?* Not ten things. One. Examples:

- "Get into stable housing with my dog by summer."
- "Stay sober one day at a time and rebuild trust with my kids."
- "Get my VA claim filed and a doctor I trust."

## 2. Commander's Intent: Why It Matters

Write down *why*. When things get hard in March, the why will carry you. For many of us, the why has a name: a child, a spouse, a dog, a buddy we lost, or God.

## 3. Objectives: Phase Lines

Break the mission into phases with dates. Housing might look like:

- **Phase 1 (January):** get ID and DD-214, enroll in VA healthcare.
- **Phase 2 (February-March):** meet a case manager, apply for HUD-VASH or SSVF.
- **Phase 3 (April-June):** find a unit that accepts my dog, move in.

## 4. Resources and Support

List what you have and what you need. Faith Forward, STL Veterans Hub, the VA, Vet Centers, SSVF programs, your parish, and your battle buddies are all assets.

## 5. Battle Buddy

**No one goes outside the wire alone.** Pick one person who will check on you weekly. Faith Forward volunteers can be that person.

## 6. After-Action Review

Every Sunday, ask: *What went right? What went wrong? What will I do differently?* Then pray over the week ahead.

## Grace for the Restart

You will miss objectives. Everyone does. God's mercies "are new every morning" (Lamentations 3:23). A new year is good, but so is a new month, a new week, a new day. You can start over at any minute.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, I hand You this new year. Forgive yesterday, guide today, and hold tomorrow. Give me one mission worth fighting for, the discipline to work the plan, and the humility to ask for help. Be my Commander. I will follow. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Missouri Veterans Commission** (573-751-3779) has Veterans Service Officers who help file VA claims for free. Never pay anyone to file a VA claim for you.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

New year, new skill: teach **"Place."** Lure your dog onto a raised cot or mat, reward when all four paws are on, then reward lying down. Build to 30 minutes. A dog with a solid place is calmer everywhere.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Write your one-page battle plan this week and share it with one battle buddy.
