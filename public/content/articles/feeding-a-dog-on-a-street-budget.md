# Feeding a Working Dog on a Street Budget

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · April 11, 2027 · Week 24*

> **"Consider the ravens: for they neither sow nor reap... and God feedeth them."**
> Luke 12:24 (KJV)

A healthy dog needs good food, and a good diet doesn't have to cost a fortune. Here's what our K9 program teaches veterans and caregivers. Every dog is different, so check with a veterinarian.

## How Much to Feed

Veterinarians estimate daily calories using a dog's **resting energy requirement**: 70 × (body weight in kg)^0.75, multiplied by an activity factor (about 1.6 for an average neutered adult, more for working or very active dogs, and more in cold weather). Our K9 Training Suite calculates this for you. Then divide by the kcal per cup on the bag.

Example: a 50-lb active pit bull may need roughly 1,250-1,500 kcal/day, around 3½-4 cups of a typical kibble.

## Read Body Condition, Not Just the Bag

Feeding guides on bags are often generous. Look and feel: you should **feel the ribs easily** under a thin layer of fat and see a waist from above. Adjust food 10% up or down every two weeks to hit that target.

## Budget Tips

- Buy the **largest bag** you can store; cost per pound drops.
- Choose a mid-range food that lists a named protein first; you don't need boutique brands.
- Use part of the daily ration as **training treats**.
- Faith Forward stocks food at outreach events and accepts donations of sealed bags.

## Storage When You Live Outside

- Keep kibble in a **sealed bucket with a lid** to block rain, ants, and rodents.
- Pop-top cans need no opener; use within a couple hours once open.
- Carry a collapsible bowl and plenty of water, especially in summer.

## Never Feed

Xylitol (some peanut butters and sugar-free gum), chocolate, grapes and raisins, onions and garlic, cooked bones, fatty scraps, and any human medications.

Make mealtime training time: hand-feed part of breakfast during a five-minute session of sit, down, and watch. Your dog works for food, bonds with you, and learns faster.
