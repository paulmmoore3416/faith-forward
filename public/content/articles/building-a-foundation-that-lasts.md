# Building a Foundation That Lasts: Stewardship and Transparency

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · October 10, 2027 · Week 50*

> **"Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful."**
> 1 Corinthians 4:2 (KJV)

Every gift to Faith Forward, whether a backpack, a bag of dog food, a rosary, or a dollar, is entrusted to us. Paul says the first requirement of a steward is to be **faithful**. Here's how we work to deserve your trust.

## Governance

Faith Forward is building a board of directors with independent members, a conflict-of-interest policy, financial controls, and regular meetings with minutes. No one person should control everything, including the founder. Good governance protects the mission.

## Financial Transparency

- Separate bank accounts and dual approval for significant expenses
- Annual budget approved by the board
- Annual reports to donors
- Required filings with the IRS and state

## Donated Items

Every in-kind donation is logged, sorted with respect, **blessed**, and distributed. We track what comes in and what goes out, and we report it.

## Privacy and Dignity

We protect the privacy of the veterans we serve. We never share photos or stories without written consent.

## Why It Matters

Scandals in charities hurt donors, and they hurt the people the charity serves. We want Faith Forward to outlive its founder and keep serving veterans for generations. That requires building a foundation, literally and spiritually, that lasts.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, everything we have is Yours. Make Faith Forward a faithful steward of every gift. Keep us honest, accountable, and humble. Protect this ministry from pride and waste, and let it outlive all of us in serving Your people. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Donors can review any charity's filings through the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search, and the Missouri Attorney General's office oversees charitable organizations in the state.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Stewardship applies to animals too: keep records. The K9 Suite's logs and reports make a dog's health and training history transparent to vets, landlords, and future handlers.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Ask us anything about how we use donations. We'll answer.
