# Faith Forward: The Mission Continues

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · October 24, 2027 · Week 52*

> **"Forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before, I press toward the mark."**
> Philippians 3:13-14 (KJV)

Our name says it: **Faith Forward**. Not faith frozen in the past, but faith moving forward. As we close our first year of weekly articles and head toward another Veterans Day, here's where we're going.

## Vision for the Year Ahead

1. **More outreach, more consistently.** Additional routes and teams across St. Louis City and County.
2. **Train the trainers.** Our founder completes the K9 curriculum and certifies the first cadre of Faith Forward trainers, many of them veterans themselves.
3. **K9 clinics.** Regular clinics offering food, prevention, basic vet referrals, training, and blessings for veterans' dogs.
4. **Deeper partnerships** with mental-health providers, housing programs, and parishes.
5. **A permanent home base**: a place for supplies, training, fellowship, and prayer.
6. **Financial sustainability** through monthly donors, grants, and church partners.

## How You Can Join

- **Give**: supplies or financial support
- **Serve**: outreach, sorting, driving, K9, prayer team
- **Partner**: churches, providers, businesses, vets
- **Pray**: for veterans, their families, and their dogs
- **Share**: follow us and tell our story

## Spiritus Invictus

Every veteran we've met this year has been wounded. Not one has been conquered. That's the promise of our crest and of the Gospel. The mission continues.

### About Faith Forward Foundation

**Faith Forward Foundation** provides assistance, resources, supplies, and religious faith-based services to displaced and homeless veterans, their families, and the animals who walk beside them in the St. Louis, Missouri area. Founded by **The Reverend Paul Moore**, a disabled three-tour Iraq veteran, ordained minister, and creator and founder of [STL Veterans Hub](https://www.stlveteranshub.com). *Spiritus Invictus*: the unconquered spirit.
