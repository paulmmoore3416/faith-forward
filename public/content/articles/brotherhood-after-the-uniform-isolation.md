# Brotherhood After the Uniform: Fighting Isolation

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · January 17, 2027 · Week 12*

> **"Two are better than one... For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth."**
> Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 (KJV)

In uniform, you were never alone. You ate, slept, trained, and fought next to people who would die for you. Then you got out, and suddenly no one had your back. For many veterans, **losing the unit hurts more than losing the job**.

## Why Isolation Is Dangerous

Loneliness isn't just sad; it's a health risk. It's linked to depression, substance misuse, and suicide. Veterans who isolate stop seeing doctors, stop calling family, and stop asking for help. The enemy wants us alone.

## Ecclesiastes Had It Right

"Woe to him that is alone when he falleth." Three thousand years ago, the Preacher understood what every infantry squad knows: **you need someone to pick you up**.

## Rebuilding Your Unit

You can't recreate your old platoon, but you can build a new one:

1. **Find a veterans group**: VFW, American Legion, DAV, Team Rubicon, The Mission Continues, or a Faith Forward fellowship.
2. **Pick a battle buddy** and set a weekly check-in.
3. **Serve.** Volunteering gives back the purpose and teamwork you lost.
4. **Join a church small group** or men's/women's ministry. The early Church was a unit: "they continued steadfastly... in fellowship" (Acts 2:42).
5. **Train a dog.** A dog gets you out of the house and into conversations with strangers.

## Reach Out to One

If you're doing okay, someone from your old unit probably isn't. Call one buddy this week. You don't need a reason. "Hey, thinking about you" might save a life.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, You did not make us to be alone. Thank You for the brothers and sisters who had our backs. Bring isolated veterans back into community. Give us courage to pick up the phone, and give us a new unit in Your Church. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Team Rubicon** and **The Mission Continues** (1141 S. 7th St, St. Louis) put veterans back to work on service missions, rebuilding purpose and brotherhood.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Walking your dog is a social bridge. Practice **loose-leash walking** this week: stop whenever the leash tightens, and start again when it loosens. A dog that walks politely makes it easy for neighbors to say hello.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Call one person from your old unit this week. No reason needed.
