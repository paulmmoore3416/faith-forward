# Your First Outreach: Volunteering With Faith Forward

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · April 25, 2027 · Week 26*

> **"Also I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here am I; send me."**
> Isaiah 6:8 (KJV)

So you want to serve on the street with us. Thank you! Here's what your first Faith Forward outreach will look like.

## Before We Go

- **Training**: a short orientation on dignity, safety, boundaries, crisis response, and dog safety.
- **Prayer**: every team prays together before rolling out.
- **Kit**: backpacks, water, socks, hygiene kits, snacks, dog food and water bowls, slip leads, resource cards, and a first-aid kit.

## How We Approach

- **Teams of at least two**, always.
- Introduce yourself by first name: *"Hi, I'm Mark with Faith Forward. Can I offer you some water or socks?"*
- **Ask before approaching anyone's dog.** Offer the dog water or food. It's a natural icebreaker.
- Ask: *"Have you ever served in the military?"*
- Listen more than you talk. Don't make promises you can't keep.

## Dignity Rules

- No photos of people, faces, tents, or dogs without written consent.
- No lecturing, no preaching at people. We **offer** prayer; we never require it.
- Use people's names. Remember them next time.

## Safety

- Stay with your team and in sight of each other.
- Trust your gut; it's okay to walk away.
- If someone is in medical danger or crisis, call 911 or 988 (press 1).

## After

We debrief, log contacts so we can follow up within a week, and pray for everyone we met by name.

## Here Am I

You don't need special skills. You need a willing heart and good shoes. Say what Isaiah said: *"Here am I; send me."*

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, here am I. Send me. Go before our outreach teams into the streets. Keep us safe, humble, and kind. Let every veteran we meet feel seen, respected, and loved, and let us see You in them. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Sign up to volunteer on the Faith Forward website's **Contact** page or message us on Facebook.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Outreach dog safety: approach from the side, crouch, let the dog come to you, and let them sniff your hand. Watch for stiffness, hard staring, or lip lifts. If you see them, back away calmly.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Sign up for orientation, or sponsor an outreach kit.
