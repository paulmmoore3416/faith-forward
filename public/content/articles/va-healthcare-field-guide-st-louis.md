# The VA Healthcare Maze: A Field Guide for St. Louis

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · February 21, 2027 · Week 17*

> **"Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you."**
> Matthew 7:7 (KJV)

For many veterans, the hardest part of VA healthcare is getting in the door. Here is a field guide for the St. Louis area. Systems change, so confirm details when you call.

## Step 1: Gather Your Documents

- **DD-214** (discharge papers). If you don't have it, you can request records through the National Archives (eVetRecs/milConnect) or ask a Veterans Service Officer for help.
- Photo ID
- Social Security number
- Insurance information, if you have any
- Income information (for some eligibility categories)

## Step 2: Apply

You can apply online at VA.gov, by phone at **877-222-8387**, or in person at a VA medical center enrollment office.

## Step 3: Know Your Facilities

- **John J. Cochran VA Medical Center**: 915 N. Grand Blvd, St. Louis (314-289-6300)
- **Jefferson Barracks VA Medical Center**: 1 Jefferson Barracks Dr, St. Louis (314-652-4100)
- **St. Louis Vet Center**: 4735 Fyler Ave (314-845-8580), counseling without needing VA enrollment
- Community-based outpatient clinics in the region

## Step 4: Use Your Advocates

- **Patient advocates** at each VA facility help resolve problems.
- **Veterans Service Officers** (Missouri Veterans Commission, DAV, VFW, American Legion) file claims for free.
- **Faith Forward** helps with paperwork, rides, and moral support, and can look after your dog during appointments when possible.

## If You're Homeless

Tell the front desk. The VA has dedicated homeless programs and can often fast-track care. Call **877-424-3838** any time.

## Persistence Is Faithful

"Knock, and it shall be opened." Sometimes you have to knock more than once. Keep knocking. Bring a buddy. Write down every name and date.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, give patience to veterans navigating paperwork and phone trees. Give compassion to every VA employee, nurse, and doctor. Open doors that seem shut, and lead every veteran to the care they earned. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**VA Health Benefits line**: 877-222-8387. **Missouri Veterans Commission**: 573-751-3779 for free help from a Veterans Service Officer.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Taking your dog to a VA appointment? Only trained service dogs have access to VA facilities. Plan ahead: Faith Forward volunteers may be able to watch your dog during appointments.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

If you're a veteran not enrolled in VA care, gather your documents this week. If you're a supporter, offer a ride to someone's appointment.
