# Heat Is Coming: Summer Safety for Veterans and Their Dogs

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · May 23, 2027 · Week 30*

> **"For thou hast been... a refuge from the storm, a shadow from the heat."**
> Isaiah 25:4 (KJV)

St. Louis summers bring brutal heat and humidity. For anyone living outside, and for their dogs, heat can be as deadly as winter cold.

## Heat Illness in People

**Heat exhaustion**: heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headache, cool clammy skin. Move to shade or AC, drink water, loosen clothing, apply cool cloths.

**Heat stroke** (emergency): high body temperature, confusion, hot red skin (may stop sweating), fast pulse, possible fainting. **Call 911.** Cool the person while waiting.

Many medications (some for blood pressure, mental health, and other conditions) increase heat risk.

## Heat Illness in Dogs

Dogs cool mostly by panting. Warning signs: heavy panting, thick drool, bright red or pale gums, stumbling, vomiting, collapse. Cool the dog with **room-temperature water** (not ice), use a fan, and get to a vet immediately.

**The pavement test:** put the back of your hand on the asphalt for five seconds. If it's too hot for you, it's too hot for paws. Walk on grass, early in the morning or after dark.

Short-muzzled breeds (like some American Bullies) are especially vulnerable.

## Cooling Centers

During heat advisories, St. Louis opens cooling centers. Ask whether pets are allowed, and call Faith Forward if they're not.

## What to Donate This Summer

- Bottled water and electrolyte drinks/packets
- Sunscreen, hats, cooling towels, lightweight socks
- Collapsible dog bowls and extra water for dogs
- Shelf-stable food that won't melt

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, be a shade from the heat for every veteran living outside this summer. Give water to the thirsty, shade to the weary, and cool ground for tired paws. Lead people to cooling centers, and send us with water. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Call **211** (United Way 2-1-1) for current cooling-center locations and hours in the St. Louis region during heat advisories.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Summer exercise for dogs: train early morning or after sunset, cut session length in half, offer water every 10 minutes, and avoid any work above a heat index of 90°F.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Donate a case of water and a pack of collapsible dog bowls, and keep extra water in your car this summer.
