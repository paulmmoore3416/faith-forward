# Training the Handler First: Lessons From the K9 Program

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · March 7, 2027 · Week 19*

> **"Let all things be done decently and in order."**
> 1 Corinthians 14:40 (KJV)

Military working dog handlers have a saying: *"It travels down the leash."* Your stress, your confidence, your consistency or inconsistency all flow straight down to the dog. That is why the Faith Forward K9 Program starts with **Phase 0: Handler Foundations**.

## Lesson 1: Read the Dog

Dogs talk constantly through lip licks, yawns, ear position, tail carriage, and weight shifts. A handler who can read stress early can prevent most bad outcomes. We teach the "ladder of aggression," from a yawn all the way up to a bite, so handlers intervene on the bottom rungs.

## Lesson 2: Timing Is Everything

Dogs learn from what happens **in the moment**. Our marker system ("YES" means reward is coming) only works if the marker lands exactly when the behavior happens. We drill timing without dogs first, saying "YES" the instant a dropped ball hits the floor, until handlers are on time 18 out of 20.

## Lesson 3: Short, Clear, Consistent

Five-minute sessions, two or three times a day, beat one long session. Same words, same hand signals, same rules, every time.

## Lesson 4: Regulate Yourself

A dysregulated handler produces a dysregulated dog. We teach handlers **box breathing** (in 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4) before every session. For veterans with PTSD, this becomes a skill that helps far beyond dog training.

## Lesson 5: Stewardship

The dog is entrusted to us by God. We use fair, humane methods built mainly on positive reinforcement, and we never train in anger.

## The Founder Goes First

Our founder is completing every lesson with Gypse before certifying anyone else. Leaders go first.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, make me a calm leader for the dog You placed in my care. Let my hands be gentle, my voice steady, my timing true. Teach me patience through this animal, and let what I learn on the leash make me a better servant of people. Amen.*

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Try the **timing drill**: have a friend drop a tennis ball, and say "YES" the instant it hits the floor. Twenty reps. When you're consistently on time, your dog will learn faster.
