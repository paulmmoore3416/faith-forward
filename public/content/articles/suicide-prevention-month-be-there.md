# Suicide Prevention Month: Be There

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · September 19, 2027 · Week 47*

> **"Bear ye one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ."**
> Galatians 6:2 (KJV)

September is **Suicide Prevention Month**. The VA's message is simple: **Be There**. Small acts of connection, like a text, a call, a visit, or an invitation, can make a difference for someone who feels alone.

## Small Acts, Big Impact

You don't have to be a counselor. Some ideas:

- Text a veteran: *"Thinking of you. How are you really doing?"*
- Invite someone to coffee, church, or a walk with your dog.
- Remember hard anniversaries: deployment dates, the date a buddy died.
- Check on someone going through a divorce, job loss, or legal trouble.

## Know the Warning Signs

Talking about wanting to die or being a burden; withdrawing; increased substance use; giving away possessions; reckless behavior; sudden calm after depression. If you notice them: **Ask, Care, Escort** (see our April article).

## Lethal Means Safety

Putting time and distance between a person in crisis and lethal means, especially firearms, saves lives. Many veterans store guns with a trusted friend or use locks during hard seasons. It's an act of strength, not weakness.

## What Churches Can Do

- Talk about mental health from the pulpit.
- Train ministry leaders in suicide prevention.
- Reject the idea that suicide means someone lacked faith.
- Care for survivors of suicide loss with compassion.

## Your Life Matters

If you're the one struggling: you are not a burden. Your story isn't over. Please call 988 and press 1.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord of life, be with every veteran who feels like giving up. Surround them with people who will be there. Give us courage to reach out and ask. Comfort families who lost someone to suicide, and let no one walk through the darkness alone. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Veterans Crisis Line**: 988, press 1 · text 838255. Free **gun locks** are often available at VA medical centers. Ask your provider.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Many veterans say their dog kept them alive. If a veteran's dog is part of their reason to stay, honor that bond. Help keep them together.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Text three veterans this week just to check in.
