# Women Veterans: Seen, Honored, Served

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · March 14, 2027 · Week 20*

> **"Strength and honour are her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come."**
> Proverbs 31:25 (KJV)

March is Women's History Month, a good time to say clearly: **women veterans are veterans**. Women have served in every American war, as nurses, spies, soldiers in disguise, pilots, and, today, in every branch and nearly every role.

## Often Unseen

Women veterans regularly report being asked whether the veteran plates belong to their husband, or being overlooked at veteran events. Many don't identify as veterans themselves. That invisibility has consequences: women may not seek the benefits and care they earned.

## Unique Challenges

- **Military sexual trauma (MST)** affects many women veterans (and many men). The VA provides free MST-related care, even without a service-connection rating.
- **Homelessness among women veterans** often looks different: couch-surfing, staying in cars, or remaining in unsafe situations, frequently with children.
- **Single parenting** and childcare barriers make appointments and jobs harder.
- **Women-specific health care** needs to be available and comfortable.

## What Faith Forward Does

We include women-specific hygiene items in every outreach supply run, connect women veterans to the VA's Women Veterans Program, and ensure safe, respectful spaces. Women veterans with children and pets are welcome in every part of our ministry.

## Honor Her

Next time you meet a woman who served, say, "Thank you for your service," and mean it. Then ask, "Is there anything you need?" Then listen.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, we lift up the women who served, often unseen and unthanked. Heal wounds no one else knows about. Surround them with safety, dignity, and sisterhood. Clothe them with strength and honor, and let them rejoice in the days to come. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Women Veterans Call Center**: **855-829-6636** (call or text). The VA also offers MST-related care at every medical center. Ask for the MST Coordinator.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Many women veterans find comfort and safety with a dog. A **"Cover"** task, where the dog sits behind the handler facing away, creates personal space in lines and crowds. It's a calming task, not a guarding one.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Donate feminine hygiene products this month, and thank a woman veteran by name.
