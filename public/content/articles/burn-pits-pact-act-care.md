# Burn Pits, the PACT Act, and the Care You've Earned

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · August 29, 2027 · Week 44*

> **"Bless the LORD, O my soul... Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases."**
> Psalm 103:2-3 (KJV)

Many veterans of Iraq, Afghanistan, and the Gulf lived and worked near **burn pits** where trash, chemicals, and equipment were burned in the open. Earlier generations faced Agent Orange and other exposures. For years, getting care for these exposures was a fight.

## The PACT Act

The **Sergeant First Class Heath Robinson Honoring our PACT Act**, signed in August 2022, is one of the largest expansions of VA health care and benefits in history. Among other things, it:

- Added many **presumptive conditions** linked to burn pits and other toxic exposures, including certain cancers and respiratory illnesses
- Expanded presumptions related to Agent Orange and radiation
- Requires VA to offer **toxic exposure screenings** to enrolled veterans
- Expanded health care eligibility for many post-9/11 veterans

## What You Should Do

1. **Get a toxic exposure screening** at your VA.
2. **Consider the Airborne Hazards and Open Burn Pit Registry.**
3. **File a claim** if you have a related condition, with a free VSO.
4. **If you were previously denied**, ask your VSO whether you can file again under the new law.

## Don't Give Up

Many veterans assumed they'd be denied, so they never applied. Don't count yourself out. Psalm 103 lists "healeth all thy diseases" among God's benefits. Seek the earthly care you earned while you pray for healing.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, heal the bodies of veterans harmed by toxic exposures: their lungs, their blood, their bodies. Guide the doctors who treat them. Give veterans persistence to seek care and justice. And comfort families who lost loved ones to these illnesses. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Learn more and apply at **VA.gov/PACT** or call **800-698-2411**. A free VSO can help you file.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Dogs can also be affected by environmental toxins: smoke, chemicals, rodent poisons. If your dog lives outside, keep them away from burn areas, chemical spills, and trash heaps.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

If you served in a combat zone, ask for your toxic exposure screening at your next VA visit.
