# Mothers Who Serve and Mothers Who Wait

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · May 9, 2027 · Week 28*

> **"Now there stood by the cross of Jesus his mother."**
> John 19:25 (KJV)

Mother's Day can be joyful and painful in the same breath. For military families, it carries layers: the mother deployed overseas missing a child's birthday, the mother at home praying through every news report, and the **Gold Star mother** whose child never came home.

## Mary at the Cross

When everyone else fled, Mary **stood** at the foot of the Cross. She did not run from her son's suffering. Christian tradition calls her *Our Lady of Sorrows*. Gold Star mothers understand her better than most of us ever will.

## Mothers Who Served

Women who are mothers and veterans balance trauma, transition, and caregiving all at once. Some face homelessness with children in tow. Faith Forward offers them supplies, prayer, and referrals, with no judgment and with respect for their service.

## Mothers of Veterans

Many mothers of veterans carry the quiet burden of watching their adult child struggle after service, with PTSD, addiction, or homelessness. If that's you: you're not alone, you didn't fail, and there is help for families, too.

## Gold Star Mothers

To every Gold Star mother: your child's sacrifice is not forgotten. We pray for them by name and for you.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Holy Mary, Mother of God, who stood at the foot of the Cross, stand with mothers of the military today: those who serve, those who wait, those who grieve. Comfort Gold Star mothers. Strengthen mothers caring for wounded children. Pray for us. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**American Gold Star Mothers** supports mothers who lost a child in military service. **Coaching Into Care** (888-823-7458) helps families encourage a veteran to seek care.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Moms with dogs and kids: teach children the dog's "safe place" (crate or mat) where the dog is never bothered. It protects both kids and dogs.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Call a military mom this week. If you know a Gold Star mother, tell her you remember her child by name.
