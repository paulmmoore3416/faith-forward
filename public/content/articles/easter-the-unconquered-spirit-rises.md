# Easter: The Unconquered Spirit Rises

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · March 28, 2027 · Week 22*

> **"He is not here: for he is risen, as he said."**
> Matthew 28:6 (KJV)

**Christ is risen! He is risen indeed!**

On Good Friday, Jesus died the death of a criminal. His friends scattered. The powerful thought they'd won. The tomb was sealed and guarded by soldiers. Then, on the third day, the stone was rolled away and the tomb was empty.

## Why Easter Is Our Motto

Faith Forward's motto, *Spiritus Invictus*, "the unconquered spirit," isn't about human toughness. It's about resurrection. The Holy Spirit who raised Jesus from the dead lives in those who trust Him (Romans 8:11). That means nothing (not war, not addiction, not homelessness, not prison, not despair) gets the final word.

## Resurrection Stories

Every week we see small resurrections:

- A veteran who lived under a bridge gets the keys to an apartment, *with his dog*.
- A man sober for one day becomes sober for a year.
- A woman who hadn't prayed since Iraq picks up a rosary from a donation bin.
- A dog who was skin and bones becomes healthy and trained, and her handler beams with pride.

These are signs of the big Resurrection, the one that guarantees ours.

## Soldiers at the Tomb

Matthew tells us Roman soldiers guarded the tomb, and they shook with fear when the angel came. Even the best-trained guard couldn't hold back God. If you feel trapped by your past, remember: the stone has already been rolled away.

## Live as Easter People

Pope St. John Paul II said, *"We are the Easter people and hallelujah is our song."* This week, live like it. Visit someone who feels entombed. Bring hope. Roll away a stone.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Risen Lord, You broke the chains of death and rolled away the stone. Raise up every veteran who feels buried: by grief, addiction, homelessness, or despair. Fill us with the unconquered Spirit. Let us live as Easter people, and let hallelujah be our song. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

If Easter is a hard day (grief often spikes on holidays), the **Veterans Crisis Line** is open: 988, press 1, or text 838255.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Spring is here: restart **heartworm and flea/tick prevention** if you paused it (in Missouri, year-round is recommended), and schedule your dog's annual exam and vaccines.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Share the Easter message with a veteran who needs hope, and invite someone to worship with you.
