# Service Dog, ESA, or Pet? A Plain-English Guide to the Law

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · January 31, 2027 · Week 14*

> **"Open thy mouth for the dumb in the cause of all such as are appointed to destruction."**
> Proverbs 31:8 (KJV)

Veterans with dogs are often told different things by different people: "You need a vest." "You need a certificate." "ESAs can go anywhere." Most of it is wrong. Here is a plain-English overview. (This is general information, not legal advice.)

## Service Dogs (ADA)

Under the Americans with Disabilities Act, a **service animal** is a dog individually trained to do work or perform tasks for a person with a disability. That includes **psychiatric service dogs** trained for tasks such as interrupting anxiety behaviors, waking a handler from nightmares, or deep pressure therapy.

- Service dogs may accompany their handlers in most public places.
- Staff may ask only two questions: **Is the dog required because of a disability?** and **What work or task has it been trained to perform?**
- No certification, ID card, or vest is required by federal law.
- A business can exclude a service dog that is **out of control** or **not housebroken**.

## Emotional Support Animals (Fair Housing)

An **emotional support animal (ESA)** provides comfort just by being there; no task training is required. ESAs do **not** have public access rights under the ADA. But under the **Fair Housing Act**, a person with a disability may request a reasonable accommodation to live with an assistance animal, usually supported by documentation from a licensed professional who knows them. Pet fees and many breed and weight restrictions generally do not apply to assistance animals, though a specific animal that poses a direct threat can be excluded.

## Air Travel

Since 2021, U.S. airlines are **not required** to accept ESAs as anything other than pets. Trained psychiatric service dogs are accepted, typically with a Department of Transportation form.

## Beware of Scams

Online "registries" and instant ESA certificates have no legal standing. Avoid them. Work with your own provider.

## Behavior Is Everything

Whatever the category, a calm, well-trained dog opens doors. That's why Faith Forward's K9 program focuses on obedience, public manners, and Canine Good Citizen preparation.
