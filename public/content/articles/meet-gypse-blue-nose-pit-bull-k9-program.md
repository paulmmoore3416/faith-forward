## About Blue Nose Pit Bulls

"Blue nose" is not a separate breed. It is a **color variety** of the American Pit Bull Terrier (and related breeds like the AmStaff) caused by a dilute gene that turns black pigment to grey-blue. Blue dogs are beautiful, but the dilute gene can come with **skin sensitivities and coat thinning** (color dilution alopecia), so they need good nutrition, omega-3s, gentle grooming, and a watchful eye.

## Pit Bull Facts and Myths

Pit bulls are often misunderstood. Here is what our trainers emphasize:

- **They are deeply people-oriented.** APBTs were historically bred to be handled closely by people, and most are affectionate, clownish companions.
- **Many can be dog-selective** as they mature, so careful socialization and management matter.
- **They are strong and determined**, which means training "leave it" and a reliable release ("out") is essential.
- **They make outstanding emotional-support dogs** thanks to their affection and love of contact.

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**AKC Canine Good Citizen (CGC)**: a 10-item test of good manners. Many landlords and insurers recognize it. Faith Forward helps veterans prepare and connects them with local evaluators.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Blue-coated dogs and sun: thin-coated areas can sunburn. Limit midday exposure in summer and ask your vet about pet-safe sunscreen for the nose and belly.
