# One Year of Faith Forward: What God Has Done

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · October 17, 2027 · Week 51*

> **"Hitherto hath the LORD helped us."**
> 1 Samuel 7:12 (KJV)

When Samuel's people won a hard-fought victory, he set up a stone and named it **Ebenezer**: *"Hitherto hath the LORD helped us."* As Faith Forward approaches one year, we're setting up our own Ebenezer.

## What God Has Done

In our first year we:

- Launched our website, K9 Handler Training Suite, and command center
- Ran outreach in every season: the Winter Survival Backpack Drive, summer heat relief, back-to-school
- Built our K9 program, starting with our founder and Gypse
- Distributed blessed backpacks, clothing, hygiene supplies, food, animal supplies, and religious items
- Formed partnerships with mental-health providers, outreach programs, and parishes
- Published 51 weekly articles and a daily presence on social media
- Most of all, met veterans by name, prayed with them, and walked with them

*(Exact numbers are reported in our annual report.)*

## What We Learned

- **Presence matters more than programs.** Showing up consistently builds trust.
- **The dogs open doors.** Offering water to a dog has started more conversations than any flyer.
- **We can't do it alone.** Partners multiply our impact.
- **Faith sustains the work.** When we're tired, prayer carries us.

## Thank You

To every donor, volunteer, partner, priest, prayer warrior, and veteran who trusted us: thank you. This is your Ebenezer too.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Hitherto hath the Lord helped us. Thank You, Lord, for every veteran served, every dog cared for, every gift given, and every prayer prayed this year. Forgive our mistakes and multiply our faithfulness. Lead Faith Forward into the year ahead. Amen.*

### About Faith Forward Foundation

**Faith Forward Foundation** provides assistance, resources, supplies, and religious faith-based services to displaced and homeless veterans, their families, and the animals who walk beside them in the St. Louis, Missouri area. Founded by **The Reverend Paul Moore**, a disabled three-tour Iraq veteran, ordained minister, and creator and founder of [STL Veterans Hub](https://www.stlveteranshub.com). *Spiritus Invictus*: the unconquered spirit.
