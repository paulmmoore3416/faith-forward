# Gratitude in the Hard Places: A Veteran's Thanksgiving

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · November 22, 2026 · Week 4*

> **"In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you."**
> 1 Thessalonians 5:18 (KJV)

Paul wrote "in every thing give thanks" while living a life of shipwrecks, beatings, prison cells, and hunger. Notice the word: **in** every thing, not **for** every thing. Nobody is asked to be thankful for PTSD, for a lost leg, or for a winter under a bridge. But even in those places, there is something to thank God for.

## The Holidays Hurt

For many veterans, the holidays are the hardest weeks of the year. The table reminds them of who is not there: the buddy who didn't come home, the marriage that ended, the kids they haven't seen. Veterans living outside watch the city fill with family traffic while they look for a warm doorway. Loneliness becomes heavier when everyone else seems to belong somewhere.

If you are a veteran feeling that weight this week, hear this clearly: **you are not alone, and you are not forgotten.**

## Gratitude as a Weapon

Gratitude is not denial. It is a discipline, like checking your weapon or your gear, and it fights despair. Researchers have found that practicing gratitude is associated with better sleep, mood, and resilience. Scripture got there first.

Try the **"Three Good Things" drill** every night this week:

1. Write or say three things that went right today, however small. *The coffee was hot. My dog slept next to me. Someone said my name.*
2. For each one, say why it happened.
3. Thank God for each one by name.

## A Seat at the Table

Faith Forward asks churches, families, and veterans organizations to set **one extra place** this Thanksgiving. Invite a veteran who would otherwise be alone. If you can't host, deliver a plate. If you can't deliver, donate non-perishable food or a grocery gift card.

And do not forget the dog. A can of food, a chew, and a warm blanket tell a veteran that someone sees their whole family.

## Our Thanksgiving

This year we are thankful for every donor, volunteer, partner, priest, and prayer warrior who helped launch Faith Forward. Above all, we are thankful for the veterans who trust us with their stories.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Father of all good gifts, thank You for the breath in our lungs and the food on our tables, whether that table is a dining room or a curb. Comfort the veterans who are alone this Thanksgiving. Bring them a knock at the door, a warm meal, and a friend. Make us thankful in every thing and generous with everything. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**St. Louis Area Foodbank** (314-292-6262) connects people to food pantries across the region. Many local pantries and parishes serve holiday meals. Call ahead for times and locations.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Thanksgiving food safety for dogs: **no cooked bones, onions, garlic, grapes, raisins, chocolate, or fatty scraps.** Fat can trigger pancreatitis. A spoonful of plain cooked turkey breast or plain pumpkin (not pie filling) is a safe holiday treat.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Set an extra place, deliver a plate, or donate non-perishable food. Then do the Three Good Things drill with your family at dinner.
