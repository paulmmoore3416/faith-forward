# Advent for the Unhoused: Waiting in the Dark for the Light

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · November 29, 2026 · Week 5*

> **"The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light: they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death, upon them hath the light shined."**
> Isaiah 9:2 (KJV)

Advent begins today, the first Sunday of a four-week season of preparation for Christmas. The Church lights one candle on the Advent wreath, then two, then three, then four. The light grows slowly, one week at a time, against the longest nights of the year.

## Veterans Know Waiting

The military teaches waiting. "Hurry up and wait" is practically scripture in every barracks. But the waiting many veterans face after service is heavier:

- Waiting for a disability claim decision
- Waiting for a housing voucher to come through
- Waiting for an appointment
- Waiting for the nightmares to stop
- Waiting for a son or daughter to call back

Waiting in the dark is exhausting. It is easy to believe nothing is happening, that God and everyone else has forgotten.

## Advent Says: Something Is Coming

Advent does not pretend the darkness is not dark. Isaiah wrote to a people walking "in the land of the shadow of death." But he promised them that **light was coming**, and it came: a child in a feed trough in a borrowed barn, born to a family far from home.

Christ entered the world as a displaced person. Mary and Joseph could not find a room. Shortly after, they became refugees fleeing to Egypt. The Holy Family knows the road our veterans walk.

## How to Wait Well

Waiting does not have to be passive. Advent asks us to **prepare**:

1. **Pray daily**, even if it is just "Come, Lord Jesus."
2. **Do the next right thing**: make the call, fill out the form, show up to the appointment.
3. **Let someone wait with you.** Faith Forward's outreach team can help you track claims, appointments, and housing applications.
4. **Light a candle**, a real one or in your heart, each week, as a sign that you have not given up.

## Four Candles of Hope

Faith Forward's Advent challenge for supporters: each week, light a candle and do one thing for a veteran. Week 1: pray. Week 2: give. Week 3: visit or call. Week 4: invite.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*O Come, O Come, Emmanuel. Lord Jesus, You were born homeless so that no homeless person would ever be outside Your love. Shine Your light on every veteran waiting in the dark: for a home, a decision, a healing, a phone call. Give them strength to wait and hope to keep going. Come quickly, Lord. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**HUD-VASH** pairs HUD housing vouchers with VA case management for homeless veterans. Ask the VA homeless program at Jefferson Barracks (314-652-4100) or any VA outreach worker how to apply.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Teach a **"Settle"** this Advent. Put a mat down, sit quietly, and reward your dog every time they lie down and relax on it. Calm is a skill. A dog that can settle anywhere gets welcomed in more places.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Start the Four Candles challenge today: pray by name for one veteran waiting on something.
