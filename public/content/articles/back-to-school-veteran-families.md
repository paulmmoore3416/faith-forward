# Back to School: Supporting Veteran Families

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · August 15, 2027 · Week 42*

> **"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it."**
> Proverbs 22:6 (KJV)

Back-to-school season is a joy for many families and a strain for others. For veteran families facing housing instability or tight budgets, backpacks, supplies, shoes, and clothes add up fast.

## Children of Veterans

Kids of veterans often carry more than school bags. Some have lived through deployments, frequent moves, a parent's injury, PTSD, or homelessness. They may be quietly brave, or quietly struggling.

## How Faith Forward Helps

Our **Back-to-School Drive** collects:

- Backpacks (the same item we give veterans!)
- School supplies: notebooks, pencils, calculators, folders
- New socks and underwear
- Hygiene items for teens
- Gift cards for shoes

Each backpack is blessed before it goes to a child.

## Education Benefits for Families

- **Post-9/11 GI Bill transfer**: eligible service members may transfer benefits to dependents (must generally be done while serving).
- **Chapter 35 (Survivors' and Dependents' Educational Assistance)** for dependents of veterans who died or are permanently and totally disabled from service.
- **State programs and scholarships** for children of veterans.

Talk to a VSO or school financial-aid office.

## Pray for Teachers and Students

Teachers often notice when a child is struggling. Pray for teachers, counselors, and students as the year begins.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, bless the children of veterans as they return to school. Give them friends, focus, and joy. Heal the worries they carry from home. Bless their teachers and protect them in their classrooms. Provide every family with what they need. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Ask the **VA Education Service** (GI Bill hotline 888-442-4551) or a VSO about Chapter 35 and transfer of benefits.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

School-year routines matter for dogs too. Keep walks and feeding times consistent, and give your dog a long-lasting chew when the kids leave in the morning to prevent separation stress.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Fill a backpack with school supplies for a veteran's child.
