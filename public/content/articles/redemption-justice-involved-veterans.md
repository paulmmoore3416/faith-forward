# Redemption After Incarceration: Justice-Involved Veterans

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · July 18, 2027 · Week 38*

> **"I was in prison, and ye came unto me."**
> Matthew 25:36 (KJV)

Our founder knows the justice system from the inside. So do many of the veterans we serve. Untreated PTSD, TBI, and substance use can lead veterans into legal trouble, and a record can make housing and jobs much harder to find afterward.

## Veterans Treatment Courts

Many jurisdictions, including in Missouri, run **Veterans Treatment Courts**, which combine court supervision with treatment, VA services, and volunteer veteran mentors. Instead of only punishing, they address underlying issues. Ask a defense attorney whether a veteran qualifies.

## VA Help for Justice-Involved Veterans

The VA has **Veterans Justice Outreach (VJO)** specialists who connect veterans in the legal system with VA services, and **Health Care for Reentry Veterans (HCRV)** for those leaving prison. Ask at any VA medical center.

## Reentry Barriers

Coming home from incarceration means facing ID problems, housing barriers, job applications with a checkbox, broken relationships, and often no support. Faith Forward helps with ID and documents, supplies, housing referrals, and community, plus the dog, if there is one waiting.

## No One Is Beyond Redemption

Moses killed a man. David arranged a murder. Paul imprisoned Christians. The thief on the cross received paradise. Grace has always been for people with records. Churches have a vital role in welcoming returning citizens without suspicion.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord Jesus, You were arrested, tried, and executed, and You promised paradise to a thief. Be with every veteran behind bars tonight. Give them hope, a path to redemption, and people waiting for them when they come home. Make our churches places of welcome. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Ask the VA for the **Veterans Justice Outreach specialist** at Jefferson Barracks or John Cochran (314-652-4100). **Missouri** courts in several jurisdictions run Veterans Treatment Courts.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Many prison dog-training programs show how training a dog can transform both the dog and the trainer. Responsibility, patience, and success are healing at any stage of life.
