# Armed Forces Day: Honoring Those Who Serve Now

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · May 16, 2027 · Week 29*

> **"Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ."**
> 2 Timothy 2:3 (KJV)

Armed Forces Day, held on the third Saturday in May, honors **those serving right now** in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and Coast Guard, active, Guard, and Reserve.

## Why Veterans Care About Active Duty

Every veteran was once a young troop. We remember the barracks, the long days, the deployments, the uncertainty. And we know the most important promise our nation makes to those who serve: **we will take care of you when you come home.**

When veterans end up homeless, forgotten, or untreated, those serving today notice. Caring for veterans tells today's service members that the promise is real.

## How to Support Service Members

- **Pray** for them by name and unit if you know them.
- **Support military families**, especially spouses holding things together during deployments.
- **Hire veterans and military spouses.**
- **Send care packages** through reputable organizations.
- **Listen** to returning service members without demanding war stories.

## A Word to Those in Uniform

Take care of your mental health now; it's a strength, not a weakness. Use chaplains and military and family life counselors. Keep your battle buddies close. When you transition, reach out early. Faith Forward and STL Veterans Hub will be here.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, protect our service members today, wherever they are. Cover them with Your hand, keep them faithful to one another, give wisdom to their leaders, and bring them home whole in body, mind, and spirit. Strengthen their families. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Military OneSource** (800-342-9647) offers free, confidential support to service members and families, including counseling. Transitioning service members can also connect with VA early through the transition program.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Military working dogs serve too. Many retire and are adopted by handlers or families. If you adopt a retired MWD, ask about their training history and medical needs, and be ready for a high-drive dog that still wants a job.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Pray for a service member by name, and support a military family in your parish.
