# Palm Sunday: The King Who Rode a Donkey

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · March 21, 2027 · Week 21*

> **"Behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass."**
> Zechariah 9:9 (KJV)

Conquering generals in the ancient world entered cities on war horses with armies behind them. On Palm Sunday, Jesus entered Jerusalem **on a borrowed donkey**, with fishermen and ordinary people waving palm branches. The crowd shouted "Hosanna!" which means "Save us!"

## Power Under Control

Veterans know power. Many of us carried weapons that could end lives in seconds. We also know that real strength is **power under control**: the discipline not to use force unless it's necessary. Jesus had all power in heaven and earth, and He chose humility.

## Servant Leadership

Within days of Palm Sunday, Jesus washed His disciples' feet, the job of the lowest servant, and said, *"I have given you an example."* The best NCOs and officers lead the same way: eat last, check on their people, do the dirty jobs alongside them.

## The Crowd Changes

The same crowd that shouted "Hosanna" on Sunday shouted "Crucify him" on Friday. Veterans know the fickleness of public praise: parades one day, forgotten the next. Jesus didn't build His identity on the crowd. Neither should we. Our worth comes from the Father, not the applause.

## Holy Week With Faith Forward

This week, join us in prayer: Holy Thursday for those who serve, Good Friday for all who suffer, and Holy Saturday for all who wait in grief. Then Easter, when everything changes.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord Jesus, humble King on a donkey, teach us to lead like You: in service, not in pride. Give us strength under control and love that washes feet. Walk with us through this Holy Week to the Cross and to the empty tomb. Hosanna! Save us! Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

Many St. Louis parishes offer Holy Week services: Palm Sunday, Holy Thursday, Good Friday, and the Easter Vigil. Faith Forward can help connect veterans with a parish or congregation of their tradition.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Power under control applies to dogs too. Practice **"Leave It"**: cover a treat with your hand, and reward with a *better* treat the moment your dog backs away. Self-control is learned.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Attend a Holy Week service, and do one act of humble service for someone this week without telling anyone.
