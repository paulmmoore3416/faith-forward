# Fireworks and Flashbacks: Getting Veterans and Dogs Through Loud Nights

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · June 27, 2027 · Week 35*

> **"I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD, only makest me dwell in safety."**
> Psalm 4:8 (KJV)

June 27 is **PTSD Awareness Day**, and July 4th is next week. For many veterans with combat trauma, and for nearly every dog, the days around the Fourth can mean anxiety, flashbacks, and sleepless nights.

## Why Fireworks Can Trigger Veterans

Sudden, unpredictable explosions and the smell of smoke can activate the same threat response the body learned in combat. Not every veteran is bothered, but many are, especially by **unexpected** blasts late at night.

## For Neighbors

- **Give notice.** Tell veteran neighbors when you plan to set off fireworks.
- **Keep it to the holiday and reasonable hours.**
- **Consider quieter options** or attend public displays.

## For Veterans

- **Plan ahead**: know when local displays are scheduled.
- **Grounding**: name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
- **White noise or music** with headphones.
- **Have a buddy on call.** 988, press 1, is there too.
- **Let your dog help**: deep pressure therapy is a powerful grounding tool.

## For Dogs

More dogs go missing around July 4th than at almost any other time of year. Protect yours:

- Keep dogs **indoors** with ID tags and an up-to-date microchip.
- Create a **safe den**: a covered crate in an interior room with white noise.
- Walk them **before dark**.
- Ask your vet *ahead of time* about anti-anxiety options.

## Peace in the Noise

Psalm 4:8 is a soldier's bedtime prayer: *"I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep."* May every veteran and every dog find that peace this week.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Lord, quiet the noise inside and outside of us. Give veterans peace when the fireworks start and calm when memories rise. Protect our animals from fear and from running away. Let us lay down in peace and sleep, for You alone make us dwell in safety. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**National Center for PTSD** (ptsd.va.gov) offers free education and the **PTSD Coach** app. **Veterans Crisis Line**: 988, press 1.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Start **noise desensitization** early next year: play firework recordings at very low volume during meals and play, increasing slowly over weeks. Never past the point where your dog stops eating.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Talk to your neighbors about fireworks courtesy, and make sure your dog's microchip info is current.
