# Labor Day: The Dignity of Work

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · September 5, 2027 · Week 45*

> **"The labourer is worthy of his hire."**
> Luke 10:7 (KJV)

Labor Day honors workers and the contributions of labor to our country. Catholic social teaching has a lot to say about work: it has **dignity** because the worker has dignity, and the economy exists to serve people, not the reverse.

## Why Work Matters to Veterans

For many veterans, losing the military means losing more than a paycheck. It's losing **identity, structure, and purpose**. Work can restore those. Unemployment, on the other hand, can feed depression, isolation, and despair.

## Work Isn't Only a Paycheck

Not every veteran can work full-time, especially those with serious disabilities. That doesn't diminish their dignity. Work can also mean volunteering, caregiving, creating, mentoring, and praying. Faith Forward invites veterans who can't hold a job to serve on outreach teams, help train dogs, or join our prayer team. Purpose isn't limited to employment.

## St. Joseph the Worker

The Church honors St. Joseph, a carpenter, as patron of workers. He worked with his hands to provide for his family, often as a refugee and immigrant. He's a great patron for veterans looking for work.

## Employers and Churches

- Offer apprenticeships and second-chance hiring.
- Connect veterans with mentors in their field.
- Churches: host job clubs and resume workshops.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*St. Joseph the Worker, pray for veterans seeking work. Lord, bless every worker with dignity, fair wages, and safe conditions. Give unemployed veterans open doors, and disabled veterans meaningful ways to serve. Let all our work honor You. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Employment Connection** (2838 Market St., 314-333-5627) runs the Homeless Veterans Reintegration Program for job readiness and placement.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Dogs love having a job too. Give your dog a "job" every day: carrying a small backpack on walks (for a healthy adult dog), finding hidden toys, or holding a place-stay while you work.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Volunteer, mentor, or hire. And if you're a veteran looking for purpose, join a Faith Forward outreach team.
