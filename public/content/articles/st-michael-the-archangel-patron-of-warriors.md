# St. Michael the Archangel: Patron of Warriors

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · September 26, 2027 · Week 48*

> **"And there was war in heaven: Michael and his angels fought against the dragon."**
> Revelation 12:7 (KJV)

September 29 is the feast of **Sts. Michael, Gabriel, and Raphael, Archangels**. St. Michael holds a special place for those who serve: he is honored as patron of soldiers, paratroopers, police officers, and first responders.

## Who Is Michael?

Michael appears in Daniel as a great prince who protects God's people, in Jude as an archangel, and in Revelation leading the armies of heaven against evil. His name means *"Who is like God?"*, a battle cry of humility. No one is like God; not Satan, not us.

## The St. Michael Prayer

Many veterans and police officers carry a St. Michael medal. The prayer, written after a vision by Pope Leo XIII, is short and powerful:

> *St. Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle. Be our protection against the wickedness and snares of the devil. May God rebuke him, we humbly pray; and do thou, O Prince of the heavenly host, by the power of God, cast into hell Satan and all the evil spirits who prowl about the world seeking the ruin of souls. Amen.*

## Spiritual Warfare Is Real

Veterans understand combat. Scripture says our deepest battle is spiritual: *"we wrestle not against flesh and blood"* (Ephesians 6:12). Despair, addiction, isolation, and shame are weapons of the enemy. But we don't fight alone, and the victory is already won.

## Gabriel and Raphael

Gabriel brought the message of the Incarnation. Raphael, whose name means "God heals," guided and healed Tobias in the book of Tobit. Raphael is a patron of healing and travelers, a fitting companion for veterans on the road to recovery.
