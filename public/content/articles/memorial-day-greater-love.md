# Memorial Day: Greater Love Hath No Man

*By The Reverend Deacon Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · May 30, 2027 · Week 31*

> **"Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends."**
> John 15:13 (KJV)

Memorial Day is not Veterans Day. Veterans Day thanks the living. **Memorial Day honors those who died in service.** For many veterans, it's the hardest day of the year: a day of names and faces.

## Greater Love

Jesus said there is no greater love than laying down your life for your friends, and then He did it. Service members who died did the same: they stood between others and danger. Their sacrifice echoes His.

## Survivor's Guilt

Many veterans carry survivor's guilt: *Why them and not me?* If that's you, hear this: your survival is not a mistake, and it is not a debt you must repay by suffering. The best way to honor the fallen is to **live**, to live well and serve others in their name.

## How to Honor the Fallen

- **Say their names.** Many veterans find healing in telling someone about a buddy they lost.
- **Visit a national cemetery.** Jefferson Barracks National Cemetery in St. Louis is one of the most significant in the nation.
- **Pause at 3:00 p.m. local time** for the National Moment of Remembrance.
- **Support Gold Star families.**
- **Pray for the dead**, a practice as old as the Church (2 Maccabees 12:46).

## For Veterans Today

If Memorial Day hits hard, don't spend it alone. Call a battle buddy. Come to a Faith Forward gathering. Or call 988 and press 1.

## 🙏 A Prayer for This Week

*Eternal rest grant unto them, O Lord, and let perpetual light shine upon them. May the souls of the faithful departed, through the mercy of God, rest in peace. Comfort the families and friends they left behind, and heal survivors who carry their memory. Amen.*

## 🧭 Veteran Resource Spotlight

**Jefferson Barracks National Cemetery** (2900 Sheridan Rd, St. Louis). Many veterans' organizations hold Memorial Day ceremonies there. The **TAPS** program (800-959-8277) supports anyone grieving a military loved one.

## 🐕 K9 Corner

Dogs have served and died in America's wars too. Many memorials honor military working dogs. A quiet walk with your own dog can be a good way to reflect on the day.

## ✅ Take Action This Week

Say the name of someone who gave their life in service, and pause at 3:00 p.m. on Memorial Day.
