# Benefits Claims Without the Runaround: Working With VSOs

*By The Reverend Paul Moore, Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · July 25, 2027 · Week 39*

> **"Let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not."**
> Galatians 6:9 (KJV)

This week marks the anniversary of the **Korean War Armistice** (July 27, 1953). Many Korean War veterans waited decades for recognition. Today's veterans shouldn't have to wait that long for benefits they earned.

## What a VSO Does

A **Veterans Service Officer (VSO)** is accredited by the VA to help veterans file claims. VSOs work for organizations like the **Missouri Veterans Commission, DAV, VFW, American Legion**, and county veterans offices. Their help is **free**.

They help you:
- File disability compensation and pension claims
- Gather evidence (service records, medical records, buddy statements)
- Prepare for Compensation & Pension (C&P) exams
- File appeals or supplemental claims

## Beware Claim Sharks

Some companies charge thousands of dollars or a percentage of your back pay to "help" with initial claims. Under federal law, only VA-accredited representatives may help with claims, and fees for initial claims are generally not allowed. **Don't pay anyone to file your initial claim.** Use a free accredited VSO.

## Tips for Success

- Keep a **claims binder**: copies of everything you submit, plus every letter you receive.
- Write down names, dates, and confirmation numbers for every call.
- Go to every C&P exam. Be honest about your worst days, not just your best.
- If denied, don't give up. Ask your VSO about review options.

## Persistence

Galatians 6:9: *"Let us not be weary in well doing."* Claims can take time. Keep going. Faith Forward can help with copies, rides to appointments, and moral support.
