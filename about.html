Our Story

Built From the Streets, Grounded in Faith

Faith Forward was born from lived experience: the cold, the hunger, the loneliness, and the grace that finds us there.

Creator & Founder

The Reverend Paul Moore

Rev. Paul Moore · Founder, Faith Forward Foundation · Creator & Founder, STL Veterans Hub

“I have been where you are. I have walked the streets of St. Louis with nothing but the clothes on my back and the weight of my service on my soul.”

Rev. Moore served three combat tours in Iraq and came home a disabled veteran. Like too many who served, he found that coming home was its own battlefield. He went through homelessness, addiction, and the justice system, and he found his way back through faith, brotherhood, and the people who refused to give up on him.

Today he serves as an ordained minister, called to the poor, the sick, and the forgotten. He founded STL Veterans Hub to map every veteran resource in St. Louis, and Faith Forward Foundation to take that map, and the Gospel, to the street.

He is also the first handler trained through the Faith Forward K9 Program, working alongside Gypse, a two-year-old blue nose pit bull, so he can train the trainers who will serve veterans and their dogs across the region.

Mission

Faith Forward Foundation provides assistance, resources, supplies, and religious faith-based services to displaced veterans, their families, and veterans in need in the St. Louis, Missouri area.

Vision

A St. Louis where no veteran sleeps unseen, unfed, or unprayed-for, and where every veteran and the animal beside them has a path home.

Our Values

✝ Faith

Christ-centered and rooted in the Church's call to serve the least of these, while welcoming veterans of every faith or none. Prayer is offered, never required.

🛡 Dignity

Every veteran is treated as the warrior and child of God they are. No judgment, no conditions on our love.

🤝 Brotherhood

Veterans serving veterans. We walk with people, not ahead of them.

🔥 Action

Faith without works is dead. We show up with backpacks, dog food, and a ride to the VA.

📜 Stewardship

Every dollar and every donated item is accounted for and used for its intended purpose.

🐾 Creation Care

The animals who stand by our veterans are part of the mission. We keep families, two-legged and four-legged, together.

Spiritus Invictus: The Unconquered Spirit

Our crest tells our story. The shield is the armor of God (Ephesians 6:11) and the protection we offer the vulnerable. The gold cross is Christ at the center of everything we do. The flame is the Holy Spirit and the fire that war could not put out. The banner reads Spiritus Invictus: the spirit that was wounded, but never conquered.

Faith Forward is proudly associated with STL Veterans Hub, also founded by Rev. Moore.
