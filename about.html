Our Mission & Founder

"I have been where you are. I have walked the streets of St. Louis with nothing but the clothes on my back and the weight of my service on my soul."

Faith Forward was founded by a combat veteran who experienced the darkest corners of post-service life: homelessness, addiction, and the justice system. After a long journey back to stability, he realized that the hardest part wasn't the desire to change—it was finding the map to do so.

Today, Faith Forward serves as that map. We aren't just a referral service; we are a brotherhood. We understand the specific needs of the St. Louis veteran community because we are part of it.
