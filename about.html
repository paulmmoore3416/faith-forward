About Faith Forward

Faith Forward is a veteran-owned and operated non-profit organization based in St. Louis, Missouri. We are dedicated to providing a helping hand to the men and women of the armed forces, past, present, and future.

Our founder, a former homeless veteran, understands the unique challenges that veterans face when transitioning back to civilian life. Having experienced homelessness firsthand, he was inspired to create an organization that not only provides immediate relief but also fosters a sense of community and hope.

At Faith Forward, we believe in the power of faith and the resilience of the human spirit. Our motto, "Invictus Spiritus," which means "unconquerable spirit," reflects our commitment to empowering veterans to overcome adversity and build a brighter future.

Through our outreach programs, we organize clothing and food drives for homeless veterans, connecting them with essential resources and a network of support. We are driven by the belief that no one who has served our country should ever feel alone or forgotten.
