Our Mission

No Veteran Left Behind. No Dog Left Behind.

Faith Forward Foundation provides assistance, resources, supplies, and religious faith-based services to displaced veterans, their families, and veterans in need across the St. Louis area. Founded by a combat veteran and ordained minister who once walked these same streets.

🎒

Street Outreach

Backpacks, clothing, hygiene kits, food and water delivered to veterans where they are: under bridges, in cars, in camps.
✝️

Spiritual Care

Prayer, scripture, pastoral counseling, sacramental referrals, and a ministry of presence for every faith tradition.
🐕

K9 Program

Most unhoused veterans have a dog. We provide food, vet care, training, and emotional-support and service-task training so they stay together.
🧭

Resource Navigation

Housing, VA healthcare, benefits, employment, food, and recovery. We do not just hand over a number; we make the connection with you.
🤝

Mental Health Partners

We partner with clinicians, counselors, and outreach programs to link veterans in crisis to care quickly.
👨‍👩‍👧

Family Support

Spouses and children serve too. We support veteran families with supplies, prayer, and referrals.

First of Its Kind

The Faith Forward K9 Handler Training Suite

A faith-based K9 program built by a three-tour Iraq veteran. It covers obedience, emotional-support and psychiatric-service tasks, detection, tracking, and protection, plus nutrition, medical care, conditioning, routines, logs, and reports. Breed profiles are preloaded for Pit Bulls of every variant, German Shepherds, Belgian Malinois, and Labrador Retrievers.

  • ✔ 8 training programs and 70+ step-by-step lessons
  • ✔ Nutrition calculator, vaccine tracking and K9 first aid
  • ✔ MWD-style daily routines, logs, reports and certificates
  • ✔ Train-the-trainer path, so veterans can train veterans

For a veteran living outside, a dog is often family, security, and the reason to keep going. Too often, shelters and housing programs force a choice between the two. Faith Forward exists so no veteran has to make that choice.
