Partnerships

Together, We Close the Gap

Faith Forward partners with mental-health providers and outreach programs to link veterans in need with care, quickly and with dignity.

💙 Mental Health Providers

Licensed counselors, psychologists, psychiatric providers, Vet Centers, and community mental-health centers. We provide warm handoffs, transportation help, and follow-up. You provide care.

🧭 Outreach & Housing Programs

SSVF, HUD-VASH, Continuum of Care, shelters, and street outreach teams. We bring supplies, faith-based support, and pet support that removes a common barrier to housing.

⛪ Parishes & Churches

Host donation drives, bless supplies, provide volunteers, open warming spaces, and connect veterans to sacraments and community.

🐾 Veterinarians & Animal Welfare

Vaccine clinics, spay/neuter, emergency care, food banks, and trainers. Help us keep veterans and dogs together.

🏢 Businesses & Employers

Sponsor backpacks, host drives, offer veteran hiring pathways, and give in-kind support.

🎖 Veteran Organizations

VFW, American Legion, DAV, and unit associations. Brotherhood multiplies when we work together. Associated with STL Veterans Hub.

How Our Referral Partnership Works

  1. Connect: We sign a simple partnership agreement (MOU) defining referral steps, consent, and contacts.
  2. Consent: With the veteran's written consent, we share only the information needed for the referral.
  3. Warm handoff: We call with the veteran, help with transportation, and look after the dog during appointments when we can.
  4. Follow up: We check in within 7 days, with the veteran's permission, to make sure the connection held.
  5. Crisis protocol: Imminent risk always goes to 988 (Press 1) or 911. We never delay emergency care.
