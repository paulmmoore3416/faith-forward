# K9 Handler Training Suite: User Guide

**Open it:** https://paulmmoore3416.github.io/faith-forward/k9-suite.html

The suite is the operating system for the Faith Forward K9 Program. It works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. Your data stays on the device you use, so **export a backup** (Data tab) regularly.

## First steps (founder + Gypse)

1. Open **Dog Profile**. Gypse is preloaded as a female blue nose American Pit Bull Terrier, about 2 years old.
2. Confirm or correct: **date of birth, weight, spay status, microchip, veterinarian**, and the **kcal per cup** printed on her food bag. These drive the nutrition math.
3. Check her **training goals**. They shape the dashboard routine and reports.
4. Click **Save Profile**.

### How lessons work

Each lesson lists the **objective, equipment, session length, steps, pass criteria, troubleshooting, safety notes,** a **breed-specific tip** for the active dog, and a **faith reflection**. Set status to *in progress* while working on it and *passed* when the dog meets the criteria. Add the instructor's name in **Signed off by**.

### About protection training (read this)

Protection work is real-world dangerous and legally serious. The suite enforces these gates:

- Dog at least **18 months**, with veterinary orthopedic clearance
- **Advanced Obedience complete**, including emergency down and call-offs
- **AKC Canine Good Citizen**
- **Professional temperament evaluation** (Lesson X1)
- A **certified helper/decoy** runs every bite session. Never act as your own decoy.
- **Liability insurance** and the legal briefing
- **K9 Program Director sign-off** recorded in the Certification tab

For **Gypse** specifically: most American Pit Bull Terriers are bred for low aggression toward people. The suite rates APBTs 2/5 for protection and recommends visible deterrence, bark-and-hold, and handler protection rather than full apprehension work, unless a professional evaluation says otherwise. A dog trained to bite on command also can't serve as an ADA psychiatric service dog. Decide which path matters most for Gypse.

## Daily rhythm we recommend

1. Morning: open the Dashboard, read the prayer, check today's routine.
2. Train 2-3 short sessions (5-15 min). **Log each one.**
3. Evening: do the daily health check (Logs → Daily Health Check).
4. Weekly: review logs; update lesson statuses.
5. Monthly: print the Monthly Progress Report; weigh the dog; export a backup.

## Using it with other veterans

Use **+ Dog** to add each veteran's dog. On shared devices, export each dog's records for the veteran and remove them from the device afterward. The **Intake Evaluation** report is a printable form for first meetings on the street or at clinics.

## Disclaimer

The suite provides training and care guidance, not veterinary or legal advice. Confirm medical and nutrition plans with a veterinarian, and legal questions with an attorney.
