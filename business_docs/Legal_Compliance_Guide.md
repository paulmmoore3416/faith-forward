# Non-Profit Legal & Compliance Checklist
*Disclaimer: This document provides a structural roadmap and is not a substitute for formal legal counsel. Please consult with an attorney specializing in Missouri non-profit law.*

## Phase 1: State Incorporation (Missouri)
- [ ] **Choose a Name:** Verify "Faith Forward" (or your exact legal name) is available in Missouri via the Secretary of State website.
- [ ] **Draft Articles of Incorporation:** Must include specific language required by the IRS for 501(c)(3) status (e.g., statement of charitable purpose, dissolution clause).
- [ ] **File Articles with the State:** Submit to the Missouri Secretary of State and pay the filing fee.
- [ ] **Appoint a Registered Agent:** Designate a physical address in Missouri where legal documents can be received.

## Phase 2: Internal Governance
- [ ] **Draft Bylaws:** Establish the rules for how the organization will be governed (board size, meeting frequency, voting procedures).
- [ ] **Develop a Conflict of Interest Policy:** Required by the IRS. Board members must disclose any financial interests.
- [ ] **Hold the First Board Meeting:**
    - Adopt the Bylaws.
    - Adopt the Conflict of Interest Policy.
    - Elect Officers (President, Secretary, Treasurer).
    - Authorize the opening of a corporate bank account.
    - Document this meeting with official Minutes.

## Phase 3: Federal Tax Exemption
- [ ] **Obtain an EIN:** Apply for an Employer Identification Number via the IRS website.
- [ ] **File Form 1023 (or 1023-EZ):** The application for 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status. 
    *   *Note: Form 1023-EZ is faster and cheaper but has gross receipt limits. If expecting significant early funding, the full Form 1023 may be required.*
- [ ] **Await IRS Determination Letter:** This document legally proves your tax-exempt status to donors and grantmakers.

## Phase 4: State Tax Exemption & Fundraising
- [ ] **Missouri State Tax Exemption:** Once the IRS letter is received, apply for Missouri state corporate income and sales tax exemption.
- [ ] **Charitable Solicitation Registration:** Register with the Missouri Attorney General's office before asking the public for donations.

## Phase 5: Operations & Compliance
- [ ] **Open a Non-Profit Bank Account:** Bring the EIN, Articles of Incorporation, and Board Resolution to the bank. **NEVER commingle personal and organizational funds.**
- [ ] **Insurance:** 
    - General Liability Insurance.
    - Directors & Officers (D&O) Liability Insurance.
    - Volunteer Accident Insurance (crucial for ground-level distribution events).
- [ ] **Annual Filings:**
    - IRS Form 990 (Annual information return).
    - Missouri Secretary of State Annual Report.
