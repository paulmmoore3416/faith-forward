# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## SOP 1: Winter Backpack & Supplies Drive Operations
**Purpose:** To standardize the collection, assembly, and distribution of survival backpacks for unhoused veterans.

### 1.1 Collection Phase
*   **Establish Drop-Off Points:** Partner with 3-5 local businesses or parishes. Provide them with branded collection bins and Faith Forward promotional flyers.
*   **Digital Campaign:** Update website and social media with drop-off locations, dates, and specifically needed items (e.g., thermal socks, hand warmers, hygiene kits).
*   **Inventory Tracking:** Log all incoming donations weekly into a centralized spreadsheet to identify shortages.

### 1.2 Packing Phase
*   **Standard Backpack Contents:**
    *   *Warmth:* 1 Beanie, 2 pairs of thermal socks, 1 pair of gloves, hand warmers.
    *   *Hygiene:* Toothbrush, toothpaste, wet wipes, deodorant, lip balm.
    *   *Sustenance:* 3 high-protein energy bars, 2 bottles of water, hydration multipliers.
    *   *Resources:* 1 laminated Faith Forward St. Louis resource map with crisis numbers.
*   **Packing Event:** Organize a volunteer assembly day. Establish stations for each category of item. Quality check each bag before sealing.

### 1.3 Distribution Phase
*   **Scouting:** Identify high-density areas for unhoused populations in St. Louis in coordination with local shelters.
*   **Safety Protocol:** Distributions must be done in teams of at least two. Teams carry emergency communication devices.
*   **Engagement:** Approach with respect. Introduce Faith Forward, offer the backpack, and ask if they need immediate help connecting to the VA or emergency shelter.

## SOP 2: Volunteer Onboarding & Management
**Purpose:** Ensure all volunteers are aligned with the mission, safe, and effective.

*   **Application:** Volunteers must fill out a basic intake form via the website.
*   **Orientation:** A brief 30-minute overview covering the mission, safety protocols, and de-escalation techniques.
*   **Code of Conduct:** Emphasize empathy, strict confidentiality regarding the veterans we serve, and adherence to safety rules.

---

## SOP 3: Crisis Intervention & Navigation
**Purpose:** Protocol for when a veteran in immediate distress contacts Faith Forward.

*   **Triage:** Assess immediate physical danger. If a life-threatening emergency, dial 911. If a mental health crisis, execute a warm handoff to the Veterans Crisis Line (988, Press 1).
*   **Resource Mapping:** For housing/food crises, immediately reference the internal Faith Forward database.
*   **Action:** Do not just give a phone number. Call the resource *with* the veteran if possible (warm handoff) to ensure they are connected to a human.
*   **Follow-Up:** Schedule a 24-hour and 72-hour check-in call with the veteran to track progress.

---

## SOP 4: Financial Accountability & Donations
**Purpose:** Ensure transparent and legal handling of funds.

*   **Receipts:** All digital donations trigger an automatic tax-deductible receipt. Physical donations of goods receive a signed acknowledgment letter.
*   **Accounting:** Utilize nonprofit accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks for Nonprofits). Reconcile accounts monthly.
*   **Board Review:** The Treasurer must present a financial health report to the Board of Directors quarterly.
